A clean office is about more than appearances. It helps staff stay healthy, makes a strong first impression on visitors, and protects your furniture, floors, and equipment. The easiest way to stay on top of it is to split the work into daily, weekly, and monthly tasks.

## Every day

These are the tasks that keep the office comfortable and presentable from one day to the next.

- Empty all bins and replace the liners
- Wipe down desks, tables, and reception counters
- Clean and disinfect door handles, light switches, and lift buttons
- Sweep and mop hard floors in entrances, corridors, and kitchens
- Clean and restock toilets, sinks, and washrooms
- Wipe kitchen counters, sinks, and the outside of appliances
- Tidy the reception and waiting areas

> **Tip:** Entrances collect most of the dust that ends up inside, especially in the harmattan season. Keeping the entrance and steps clean every day reduces cleaning everywhere else.

## Every week

- Vacuum carpets and rugs thoroughly, including under desks
- Dust shelves, window ledges, picture frames, and skirting boards
- Clean the inside of the office fridge and throw out old food
- Wipe down chairs, including armrests and bases
- Clean glass doors, partitions, and interior windows
- Disinfect shared phones, keyboards, and meeting-room equipment

## Every month

- Clean external windows and glass frontage
- Dust high areas such as air-conditioner vents, light fittings, and the tops of cabinets
- Deep clean washrooms, including tiles and grout
- Clean behind and under heavy furniture and appliances
- Wash or spot-clean upholstered furniture
- Check and clean the bins themselves, not just the liners

## Every few months

Some jobs only need doing a few times a year, but they make a big difference:

1. Deep clean carpets
2. Strip and polish hard floors
3. Clean blinds and curtains
4. Do a full declutter of storage rooms and archives

## Doing it yourself or using professionals?

Small offices can often manage daily tidying themselves, but regular professional cleaning keeps standards consistent and frees your team to focus on their work. A professional crew also brings the right equipment for jobs like high windows, floor polishing, and deep cleaning washrooms.
