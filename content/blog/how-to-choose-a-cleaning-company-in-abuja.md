Letting a cleaning company into your home or workplace is a matter of trust. A good team saves you hours every week and keeps your space healthy. The wrong one can leave you with missed corners, damaged surfaces, or worse. Whether you need a one-off deep clean or a regular contract, these are the things worth checking before you book.

## 1. Is the company properly registered?

A registered business is accountable. In Nigeria, a limited company has an RC number from the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), and a reputable cleaning company should be happy to share it. It is a simple first filter that separates established businesses from casual arrangements.

## 2. Are the staff trained and supervised?

Cleaning well is a skill. Different surfaces such as marble, glass, wood, and stainless steel each need the right products and methods. Ask whether the cleaners are trained, whether they work under a supervisor, and whether the same team will come back for repeat visits.

> **Tip:** Uniformed staff are a good sign. They show the company takes responsibility for who it sends to your door.

## 3. Do they bring their own equipment and products?

Most professional companies arrive with everything needed for the job. That means you are not left buying supplies, and the team can use the right tools for each task. If you have allergies, children, or pets, ask what products they use and whether gentler, eco-friendly options are available.

## 4. Will you get a clear quote before work starts?

Pricing depends on the size of the space, its condition, and the type of cleaning. A trustworthy company will ask questions, or look at photos or the site itself, and then give you a clear price **before** any work begins. Be careful with prices that change once the job is under way.

## 5. What kind of spaces do they already clean?

Experience matters. A team that already cleans demanding spaces, such as busy offices, short-let apartments, or high-traffic public buildings, is used to working to a standard and a schedule. Ask what kinds of clients they serve.

## 6. How easy are they to reach?

When you need a same-day clean, or you want to change a booking, you need fast answers. Check how quickly the company responds by phone or WhatsApp. Their response before you book is usually a good guide to the service you will get afterwards.

## 7. What happens if something is missed?

Even the best teams occasionally miss a spot. Ask what happens if you are not satisfied. A professional company will want to put it right.

## A quick checklist

- Registered company with an RC number
- Trained, uniformed, and supervised staff
- Brings its own equipment and products
- Clear quote before work starts
- Experience with spaces like yours
- Quick replies by phone or WhatsApp
- Willing to fix anything missed
