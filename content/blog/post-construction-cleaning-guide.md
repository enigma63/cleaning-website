The builders have packed up and your new or renovated space is finally finished. But before you move in, open for business, or hand the keys to a tenant, there is one more job: getting rid of all the dust and debris that construction leaves behind.

Post-construction cleaning is very different from a normal clean. Fine cement and plaster dust settles on every surface and works its way into corners, cupboards, and vents. Paint splashes, sticker residue, and leftover materials need careful removal so that new finishes are not scratched or damaged.

## What a post-construction clean includes

### Removing debris

- Clearing leftover offcuts, packaging, and small debris
- Sweeping out rooms, balconies, stairways, and entrances

### Dust, top to bottom

- Dusting walls, ceilings, light fittings, and fans
- Cleaning inside and on top of cupboards, wardrobes, and shelves
- Wiping door frames, window frames, skirting boards, and sockets
- Vacuuming and cleaning air-conditioner vents and grilles

### Glass and windows

- Removing stickers, labels, and protective film
- Cleaning paint and cement splashes off glass and frames
- Polishing windows, mirrors, and glass doors

### Floors

- Removing paint drips and grout haze from tiles
- Vacuuming and mopping all hard floors
- Cleaning stairs, steps, and outdoor paving

### Kitchens and bathrooms

- Cleaning cabinets, worktops, sinks, and taps
- Removing residue from tiles, showers, and toilets
- Wiping down new appliances inside and out

## Why it is worth doing properly

Construction dust is very fine. If it is not removed properly, it keeps resettling for weeks, gets into air conditioners, and can irritate eyes and lungs. Using the wrong methods on new surfaces, such as scrubbing glass with grit still on it, can leave permanent scratches. A thorough, careful clean protects the investment you have just made.

## How to prepare

1. **Wait until the trades have finished.** Cleaning before the last painter or electrician leaves usually means cleaning twice.
2. **Clear out large waste.** Big rubble and heavy materials are normally removed by the contractor before cleaning begins.
3. **Make sure water and power are on.** The cleaning team will need both.
4. **Share any special finishes.** Let the cleaners know about marble, natural stone, or delicate surfaces so they use the right products.
